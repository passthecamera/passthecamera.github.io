Pass the Camera

We hand your guests the camera, then hand you the photos.

A simple booth your guests open on their own phone. They take a few photos, you get every one of them in a folder — no group chats, no fifteen phones to collect from afterward.

Built for weddings, receptions, and gatherings where the best photos are the ones nobody official was there to take.

What we do

Every event has a hundred small moments an official photographer never catches — the cousin's toast, the aunties dancing, the in-between laughs. Those live on guests' phones and usually stay there.

We set up a link your guests open in their own browser. It opens their camera, lets them take a few photos, and sends each one straight to you — sorted, timestamped, and ready to look through the next morning.

How it works

01

Share the link

Put it on a card at the table, or send it in the event group. No app to install.

02

Guests take photos

They review each one before sending — only the ones they're happy with go through.

03

You get the folder

Every accepted photo lands in one place, organized by who sent it and when.
